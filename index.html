ABOUT FAJAR

The Dawn of Transformation

FAJAR Foundation, established in 2024 and registered under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013, is a non-profit deeply rooted in rural India.

More than an organization, FAJAR is a movement — a collective of young professionals committed to those on the margins.

"Fajar" means Dawn — the beginning of a new era
